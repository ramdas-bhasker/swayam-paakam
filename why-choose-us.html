HEALTH & PURITY FIRST

A Strict Commitment to Cooking Without Palmolein Oil

Many quick-service tiffin centres rely on heavy palmolein oil for frying vadas, pooris, and snacks. At SWAYAMPAAKAM, we have made a conscious pledge: no dish in our kitchen is ever prepared with Palmolein oil.

Instead, we use clean, high-grade cooking oils and pure cow ghee so our tiffins feel light, digestible, and truly home-like—even when enjoyed every day.

  • 100% Free from Palmolein Oil
  • Fresh Oil Batches & Clean Frying
  • Pure Cow Ghee Roasts & Tadkas
  • Light & Wholesome on the Stomach
CLEAN KITCHENS, HONEST SOURCING

Hygienic Preparation & Carefully Selected Ingredients

From the quality of sona masoori rice, urad dal, and whole spices to the filtered water used for soaking and steaming, every raw ingredient at SWAYAMPAAKAM undergoes strict quality checks.

Our kitchen staff follow disciplined sanitation routines, temperature controls, and clean-surface standards throughout morning tiffins, lunch rice meals, and evening snack service.
